How it works

Brand-new Microinverter technology sends energy ‘backwards’ into your outlet into your home’s existing power lines. This energy is consumed immediately by your fridge, router, laptop or anything else plugged in during sunlight hours before they pull electrons from the power company - so that the devices need less power from the power company.
